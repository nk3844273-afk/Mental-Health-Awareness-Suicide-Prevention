What's on your mind today?

Recognising Warning Signs

Withdrawal from friends/family, drastic mood swings, giving away possessions, talking about hopelessness, or sudden calmness after depression can be warning signs.

How to Help Someone

Listen without judgment. Ask directly about suicidal thoughts — it helps. Remove access to lethal means. Stay with them and connect them to professional help immediately.

Self-Care Strategies

Regular sleep, balanced nutrition, physical activity, mindfulness meditation, journaling, and maintaining social connections are proven mental health boosters.

Breaking the Stigma

Mental health conditions are medical issues, not weaknesses. Open, empathetic conversations reduce stigma and encourage people to seek the help they deserve.

Talking to a Professional

Therapists, counsellors, and psychiatrists are trained to help. Asking for help is a sign of strength. Many services are available online, by phone, or in-person.

Understanding Depression

Depression is more than sadness. It affects sleep, appetite, concentration, and energy. It's treatable with therapy, medication, lifestyle changes, or a combination.
